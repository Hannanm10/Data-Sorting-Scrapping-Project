Sorting Visualizer

Project Overview

The Sorting Visualizer is an interactive web-based tool that sorts a dataset of 25,000 entries using 10 different algorithms.

It helps users understand how sorting algorithms work visually, allowing real-time comparisons of speed and efficiency.

Ideal for students, educators, and enthusiasts looking to deepen their understanding of sorting mechanisms.
